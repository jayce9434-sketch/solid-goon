How to Play

MoveLeft joystick, WASD, or arrow keys.
AimDrag the right half of the screen, use mouse aim, or let auto-aim acquire targets.
FireHold FIRE or mouse. Your weapon keeps firing at your aim point.
DashDASH grants brief invulnerability. Later upgrades turn it into a weapon.
BuildEvery cleared wave opens the workbench. Buy several upgrades if you can afford them.
SynergiesUpgrade tags interact. Critical, burn, drone, armor, dash and multishot builds can scale hard.
BossesBoss waves have unique attacks, phase changes and dedicated music.
ProgressionRuns award credits, XP and crates. Spend credits on permanent systems and collect skins.
EventsSeasonal enemies drop event currency. Event bosses pay much more and unlock limited skins.
CratesEarn them from bosses and run milestones. Crates contain credits, XP, boosts and skins.
